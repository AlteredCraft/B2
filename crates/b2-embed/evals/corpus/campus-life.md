title: First term at university
---
The first weeks at a university are mostly logistics wearing the costume of
independence. Freshers collect a student card, work out which lecture theatre
hides behind which archway, and learn that the campus map lies about walking
times. Halls of residence run on a cycle of fire-drill tests and shared
kitchens; societies pitch themselves at a noisy fair, and the debating club
always has the longest queue. Seminars are where the real teaching happens — a
dozen students around one table, expected to have done the reading. By the
third week the university stops feeling like a museum being visited and starts
feeling like a place someone merely lives.
