title: Chess openings
---
The first dozen moves decide what kind of game it will be. Classical advice
compresses to three rules: fight for the center squares, develop knights and
bishops before moving the same piece twice, and castle early so the king is
tucked away before the middlegame opens lines. Memorized variations run deep
in club play, but understanding the pawn structure each opening leaves behind
matters more than recalling move twelve — the structure says which side of
the board your plans belong on long after the book moves run out.
