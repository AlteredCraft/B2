title: Stain removal
---
Speed beats chemistry: almost anything comes out if treated before it dries,
and almost nothing does after a trip through a hot dryer sets it. Blot from
the edge inward rather than rubbing, which only drives the mark deeper into
the weave. Protein marks — blood, sweat — want cold water first, since heat
cooks them into the fabric; ink yields to rubbing alcohol dabbed through the
cloth from behind; red wine lifts with salt while still wet. When the care
label allows it, an afternoon of direct sunlight bleaches out what the wash
cycle leaves behind.
