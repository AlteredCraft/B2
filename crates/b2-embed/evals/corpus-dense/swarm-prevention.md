type: note
title: Swarm prevention
---
Every swarm-control trick attacks the same two triggers: congestion and an
ageing queen's fading pheromone. Add supers before the bees need them so the
nectar has somewhere to go besides the brood nest. Open the brood nest by
checkerboarding — alternating drawn comb with empty frames — so the queen
never runs out of laying room. When queen cups are already charged, make a
split: move the old queen, two frames of brood, and the shaking bees to a new
box, and the parent hive requeens itself from the cells it built — the swarm
has effectively happened, into your equipment instead of a neighbour's
chimney. Done early, a split costs a month of buildup; done late, it merely
picks which half you keep.
