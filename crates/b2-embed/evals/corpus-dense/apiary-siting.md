type: note
title: Apiary siting
---
Site hives with the entrance catching morning sun — foragers start an hour
earlier — and a windbreak at their backs; afternoon dappled shade helps in
hot summers. Bees need water from the first warm day, and they imprint on
the nearest reliable source, so give them a dripping bucket of wet gravel
before they discover the neighbour's pool. Raise boxes on a stand: dry
bottom boards, fewer ants, and a back saved from lifting at ground level.
Face the flight path across open ground, or hedge the apiary so bees climb
above head height before crossing where people walk. Room to stand behind
each hive matters more than symmetry — every inspection happens from the
back, and a site you cannot work is a site you stop working.
