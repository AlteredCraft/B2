type: note
title: Hive inspection
---
A routine inspection answers four questions in ten minutes: is the queen
laying, is the brood pattern healthy, do the bees have room, and is anything
robbing or infesting the hive. Crack the propolis seal gently, work from the
outer frames inward, and look for eggs standing upright in cells — one per
cell means the queen was here within three days, no need to spot her. A solid
slab of capped worker brood is healthy; a shotgun-scattered pattern hints at a
failing queen or disease. Note stores, space, and queen cups along the bottom
bars, and close up before chilling the brood. Every other week in season is
plenty; each opening costs the colony a day of work re-sealing.
