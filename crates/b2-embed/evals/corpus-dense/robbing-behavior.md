type: note
title: Robbing behavior
---
Robbing is bees stealing honey from a weaker hive, and once it starts it is
nearly unstoppable: fighting at the entrance, bees wrestling in pairs on the
landing board, a frantic zigzag flight around every seam of the hive, wax
cappings littering the floor. It ignites in a nectar dearth — late summer,
usually — from any exposure: a super left open too long, syrup dribbled
outside, an entrance too wide for a small colony to defend. Reduce entrances
to a finger's width in a dearth, screen the weak hive for a day if a frenzy
starts, and drape a wet sheet over it so returning robbers give up. The
colony that starves from being robbed out was usually the one fed carelessly
in the open a week before.
