type: note
title: Hive types
---
The Langstroth is the default for a reason: stacked boxes of standardized
removable frames, sized around the bee-space gap that keeps comb where the
frames are, with a worldwide market of compatible parts and extractors. The
top-bar hive trades that for a single horizontal box at working height —
bars instead of frames, comb hanging free, no heavy lifting and no
extractor, harvested by crushing the comb. The Warré stacks small square
boxes added underneath, aiming to let bees build down as they would in a
hollow tree, opened rarely. The honest advice for a first colony is the
boring one: whatever the neighbours run, because borrowed frames of brood,
shared equipment, and comparable inspections all assume the same box.
