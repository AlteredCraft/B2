type: note
title: Honey harvest
---
Take only frames that are three-quarters capped — the wax seal is the bees'
own declaration that the moisture is low enough not to ferment; a
refractometer reading under eighteen percent confirms it. Clear the bees with
an escape board or a gentle shake and brush, and keep every super covered on
the walk back: open honey in the apiary in a dearth starts a robbing frenzy
in minutes. Indoors, slice the cappings off with a hot knife, spin the frames
in an extractor, and strain — never fine-filter — into buckets to settle.
Return the wet supers to the hives at dusk for the bees to clean and recycle
the drawn comb, the most valuable thing a colony builds.
