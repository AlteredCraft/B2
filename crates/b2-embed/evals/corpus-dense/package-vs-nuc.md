type: note
title: Package vs nuc
---
The two ways to start a colony arrive very differently. A package is three
pounds of loose bees and a caged, unrelated queen, shaken into your
equipment: cheapest, available early, installable in any hive style, but it
starts from zero — no comb, no brood — and the bees must accept the queen
through a candy plug over several days. A nucleus colony is a small working
hive on four or five frames: a laying queen already accepted, brood in all
stages, stores, and drawn comb, weeks ahead of a package by the main flow —
at a higher price, tied to the frame size it was built on. Either way, a new
colony spends its first season building, and takes feeding through every
gap in the flow.
