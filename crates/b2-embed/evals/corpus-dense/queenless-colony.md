type: note
title: Queenless colony
---
A colony that loses its queen tells you within the hour: the roar at the
entrance rises a pitch, and inspection finds no eggs, then emergency queen
cells torn into the face of worker comb around larvae young enough to
convert. Left queenless too long the fallback fails — with no brood pheromone
some workers' ovaries switch on, and laying workers dot cells with multiple
eggs on the walls, all destined to be drones. The test and the cure are the
same frame: give them eggs and day-old larvae from another hive. If they draw
queen cells on it, they knew they were queenless and are fixing it; if they
ignore it, a queen is probably present but failing, which is a different
problem with a different remedy.
